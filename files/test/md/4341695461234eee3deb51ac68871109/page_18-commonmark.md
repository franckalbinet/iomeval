All interviewed national government officials confirmed the importance
of IOM’s activities in shaping migration policies and strengthening
institutions. In Somalia, for example, IOM supported the development of
required regulations to implement the revised Immigration Act passed in
March 2023. As one national government representative stated, “IOM is
the one who has been really supporting all along from its inception to
when \[the Act\] was signed and approved under the BMM.” Similarly,
another national stakeholder confirmed that the programme was well
aligned with national priorities, particularly in migration governance
and counter-trafficking: “The program was designed and focused on
regional collaboration but aligned also with national priorities.”
Awareness-raising efforts were also a key aspect of the programme’s
relevance. One national stakeholder highlighted the importance of
community engagement: “The support in terms of creating awareness to the
local leadership and local communities on migration along these routes,
to me, that is a priority.”

IOM activities were also crucial in facilitating regional cooperation.
By addressing inconsistencies in migration policies and border
management, as well as lack of cross-border cooperation, it enabled more
coordinated responses to transnational challenges. As one stakeholder
noted, “The BMM project was instrumental in bringing the governments in
this region together so that they can actually informally have a
dialogue on issues of migration governance.” IOM activities also helped
to mobilise other actors in the migration sector, especially through
actions under SO2 focused on the training of law enforcement officials
and border authorities, and the creation or strengthening of
cross-border coordination bodies.

## 3.1.2. Ability to respond to changing contexts … page 18

The programme’s and IOM flexibility allowed it to adapt to shifting
political and security dynamics, ensuring continued relevance throughout
its implementation. However, challenges such as limited resources for
protection and reliance on external funding remain areas requiring
attention in future phases.

The dynamic nature of migration trends and political shifts in the
region required the programme to be adaptable. Country office IOM teams
worked closely with governments to ensure relevance, adjusting
activities based on emerging needs. An IOM staff member explained:
“Thanks to the flexibility and the regular discussions on the work plan,
we were able to make sure that BMM was relevant throughout Phase III.” A
key role in this regard was played by the IOM Regional Office which
ensured adaptive management of the overall IOM activities under BMM
Phase III and responded to various internal and external inquiries in a
swift manner.

changing government priorities and security situations.²¹ For instance,
the outbreak of the conflict in Sudan had profound impact on the
implementation of BMM Phase III in South Sudan, as South Sudan received
an increased number of returning South Sudanese nationals, Sudanese
refugees, and third country nationals fleeing Sudan. For the first month
