

- In Kenya, IOM facilitated multiple consultative forums for the NCM to
  advance the National Migration Policy (NMP) and the National
  Implementation Plan for the Global Compact for Migration, while also
  addressing cross-border issues through the development of a first
  draft of an Integrated Border Management (IBM) policy. Additionally,
  IOM rolled out a Community of Practice (COP) in counties like Nairobi,
  Mombasa, Lamu, and Kisumu to tackle trafficking, migrant protection,
  and policy harmonization. Organizational workshops and meetings were
  held to enhance migration governance, culminating in the revision of
  Kenya’s Migration Profile and the development of the National
  Migration Coordination Directorate.
- In Somalia, IOM assisted the Federal Government in developing a second
  draft of the NMP, integrating feedback from various stakeholders and
  ensuring alignment with global migration governance frameworks. IOM
  also supported the Immigration and Citizenship Agency (ICA) in
  developing regulations for the Immigration Act and the ICA
  Establishment Law. As one national official put it, “Without IOM, this
  thing \[the regulations\] would not have a life and would not even
  exist.” Additionally, IOM organized training sessions for ICA
  officials to improve understanding and operationalize the IBM
  Committees (IBMCs).
- In South Sudan, IOM organized meetings for government officials to
  strengthen migration governance aspects and supported the ratification
  of international conventions to combat trafficking in persons and
  smuggling of migrants. For instance, IOM supported two
  Inter-Ministerial Summits on Migration Governance in South Sudan
  during November 2023 and December 2024 following a NCM meeting. IOM
  also initiated the procurement of server room and networking equipment
  for launch of a migration database housed under the National Bureau of
  Statistics and handed over a vehicle to support its work, Additionally
  IOM conducted training for immigration officers to support the
  implementation of a National Border Strategy developed through BMM II.
- In Uganda, IOM supported the finalization of the draft National
  Migration Policy, contributed to workshops to review and validate it,
  and initiated discussions regarding the NMP roadmap and harmonization
  with other national policies. Additionally, IOM supported the Ministry
  of Internal Affairs with developing and launching a National
  Trafficking in Persons (TiP) database at the central level. IOM
  organized community-based dialogues to raise awareness about TiP and
  protect vulnerable children. Furthermore, IOM supported government
  officials in the October 2024 Africa Regional Review of the GCM,
  contributing to Uganda’s migration governance efforts. A consultant
  was onboarded to lead the development of Uganda’s GCM National Action
  Plan. Since December 2023, IOM has strengthened NCM support through
  staff secondments, quarterly meetings, training sessions, and a
  Training Package. Finally, IOM conducted a handover session to the
  Ministry of Gender Labour and Social Development of two pivotal
  publications drafted under BMM II (the Pre-departure Orientation
  Curriculum and the Migrants’ Handbook).
