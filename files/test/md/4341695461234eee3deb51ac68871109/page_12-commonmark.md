

## 1.3 EVALUATION BACKGROUND … page 12

This evaluation focuses on IOM activities within the BMM Phase III and
does not include an evaluation of the work of other BMM partners on
their components. It is a final evaluation with the objective of
examining and reviewing the results achieved by IOM’s BMM
interventions.¹⁴ It is also an external evaluation considering that two
external consultants were recruited to conduct the evaluation.

The evaluation was conducted for use by the IOM BMM project management
team to assess the results of IOM activities within the BMM Phase III,
document key lessons learned and best practices from a complete set of
activities and provide recommendations to inform the development of a
fourth phase of BMM. The evaluation examines the success of
implementation across each of the above-mentioned three components.

In addition, the evaluation considered BMM specific cross-cutting themes
and their integration in the programme design, implementation, and
reporting. These themes are gender, human rights, peace and security,
participatory development and good governance, poverty orientation and
migration and climate change.

This evaluation covers the entire implementation period of the BMM
programme’s third phase (1 October 2022 to 31 March 2025, all six
countries, and all its stages, including programme conceptualization,
stakeholders’ engagement, implementation of the activities, and project
reporting. The evaluation does not cover the first phase and second
phases of the BMM, which were between April 2016 and October 2022.

The intended audience of the evaluation includes IOM, government
authorities which acted as counterparts of the Programme, the civil
society organizations (CSOs) involved in implementing the programme and
beneficiaries, including local government authorities and other
stakeholders.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ A final, or terminal, evaluation is undertaken at the end, or close
to the end, of an intervention to examine the overall performance and
achievement of results, also for the benefit of stakeholders not
directly involved in the management and implementation of the
intervention (such as donors and governmental entities) (IOM Monitoring
and Evaluation Guidelines).
