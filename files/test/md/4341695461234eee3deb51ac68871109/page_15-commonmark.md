

### 2.2.1. Desk research … page 15

The first step of data collection was **desk research**, which included
a review of all available programme documentation. IOM provided the
evaluation team with all relevant documentation and information.
Additionally, the team has collected background documentation on
migration trends, flows, and statistics in the region, and the main
challenges faced by migrants, returnees, and communities.

Desk research was implemented in two phases:

- **Initial desk research** was carried out in the inception phase to
  help the evaluation team better understand the initiative. Desk
  research was used to re-create the Intervention Logic and prepare a
  preliminary list of interviewees for the data collection stage.
- **Desk research for data collection and analysis** was carried out
  after the approval of the Inception Report. During this phase, the
  evaluation team focused on the specific questions set out in the
  evaluation grid and focus on the triangulation of the primary data
  sources.

### 2.2.2. Interviews … page 15

The interview programme comprised the largest data collection effort of
the evaluation. The evaluation team identified several groups of
interview respondents to be included in the evaluation, based on a
purposive sampling approach. Namely, the evaluators spoke with the
stakeholders who were most engaged in the programme and who could
therefore provide the most detailed feedback on its achievements. As
limitation, this left out the opinions of less engaged stakeholders and
opportunity to explore why they were less engaged.

The evaluators conducted **40 interviews with IOM staff, government
stakeholders and CSOs** who participated in the implementation of the
programme, at both national and regional levels, in BMM implementing
countries. All countries (Djibouti, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan,
Ethiopia and Uganda) and stakeholder groups were covered. Interviews
also covered all other partner organisations implementing BMM, i.e.,
GIZ, CIVIPOL, British Council and UNODC. The following table includes
the number of interviewed respondents per organization and country,

## 2.3. DATA ANALYSIS AND LIMITATIONS … page 15

**Qualitative data** was collected from desk research, and interviews.
Interviews were summarised in writing and anonymised. **Quantitative
information** was collected through desk research (programme statistical
data). All data collected was **triangulated**, meaning that data from
different sources was analysed together and used to answer an evaluation
question. This ensured enhance validity and reliability of the findings
as the findings from one data source were tested and validated using
data from other sources.
