These efforts culminated to trainings for 60 participants in Hargeisa and Bossaso on technical and vocational education and training (TVET).

- In South Sudan, IOM held a workshop to raise awareness about human trafficking and conducted assessments of migrant protection services at border points, revealing gaps in GBV services and the lack of referral mechanisms for VoTs. IOM conducted 8 trainings were conducted for frontline protection actors on TiP, SoM, deploying VoT screening tools and improving case management reaching 251 participants (185 male and 66 female) across the country. IOM also facilitated voluntary return support for 25 migrants from various nationalities.
- In Uganda, IOM launched and handed over the National TiP Database, strengthening case management and referral for VoTs. Awareness campaigns engaged over 1,200 participants at the Uganda-South Sudan border and over 900,000 viewers through televised discussions. Trainings for first responders and border officials improved victim protection and migration governance. Additionally, IOM worked with the Ministry of Health to integrate trafficking considerations into MHPSS services.

Overall, all countries benefited from IOM activities related to SO3 under BMM Phase III, even if to a different degree. According to the results monitoring framework, four indicators were fully or partially achieved, two exceeded, and four could not be confirmed at the time of the evaluation as GIZ was noted to be responsible for the measurement of attained value (see Table 3).
