## 3.3.4. Factors influencing the programme’s effectiveness ... page 32

The evaluation identified that the implementation of IOM’s activities under the BMM Programme Phase III was shaped by a combination of factors: security challenges, political dynamics, stakeholder coordination, financial constraints, and logistical hurdles. While these challenges posed significant risks to timely implementation, IOM employed adaptive strategies, stakeholder engagement, and flexible planning to maintain progress.

### Security challenges ... page 32

Security issues posed barriers to timely implementation across several countries. According to IOM’s interim reports, in Ethiopia, the six-month state of emergency declared in August 2023 due to conflict in Amhara restricted movement, delaying activities such as Organizational Capacity Assessments (OCAs) in the region. The renovation of the Metema Border Crossing Point was delayed and subsequently cancelled due to insecurity caused by armed groups, rendering the site inaccessible. In Kenya, widespread protests disrupted the Humanitarian Border Management assessment, requiring rescheduling and enhanced risk mitigation. Similarly, in Somalia, the rollout of immigration regulations and IBMC activities faced delays due to political instability and Parliament recess. IOM addressed these challenges through risk assessments, stakeholder engagement, and flexible timelines, ensuring activities resumed when conditions allowed.
