The analysis relied on **contribution analysis**, as the evaluators aimed to establish links between the activities and inputs of IOM and BMM to the measured outcomes, while subsequently identifying other factors/actors that may have influenced the achievement of outcomes.

While the evaluators have taken all possible steps to ensure the quality of the report and its content, the evaluation faced some **limitations**. Time and resource limits did not allow for field visits to the six countries, nor for interviews with beneficiary migrants themselves. This was mitigated through online interviews with national government respondents from all BMM participant countries, who were asked about the programme’s relevance, effectiveness and impact for migrants.
