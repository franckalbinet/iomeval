### 3.3.3. Specific Objective 3: Protection services for Victims of Trafficking and of vulnerable migrants are improved at local, national, and regional levels ... page 28

Specific Objective 3 of the BMM programme aims to improve protection services for VoTs and vulnerable migrants at local, national, and regional levels. This objective is to be achieved by aligning efforts with existing regional initiatives and adhering to international obligations and standards. The approach includes enhancing access to information on safe, orderly, and regular migration, improving access to protection facilities and services, and conducting training and capacity-building activities focused on key aspects of migrant protection.

According to data collected, IOM has contributed to improved access to information and protection facilities and services for migrants.
