## 3.3.5. Integration of cross-cutting themes ... page 35

IOM demonstrated varying degrees of integration of cross-cutting themes in its design, implementation, and reporting. **Gender and human rights** considerations were well integrated through targeted training sessions, victim protection initiatives, and capacity-building efforts. For example, IOM conducted Gender-Based Violence risk mitigation and response training, ensuring that frontline protection actors were equipped to identify and support victims. Additionally, the programme incorporated human rights principles in counter-trafficking efforts, focusing on victim-centered approaches, legal protections, and case management for VoTs.

The programme also addressed **peace and security** by strengthening border management mechanisms and regional cooperation. The establishment and operationalization of IBMCs, combined with cross-border coordination meetings and study tours, contributed to enhanced migration governance and security frameworks. **Participatory development** and **good governance** were promoted through multi-stakeholder consultations and policy development support, particularly in Uganda and Ethiopia. However, the integration of poverty orientation and migration and **climate change** was less pronounced, with limited direct programming addressing climate-induced migration or economic drivers of irregular migration.
