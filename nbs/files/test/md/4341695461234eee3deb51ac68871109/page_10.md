Compared to the previous phases, the country scope of Phase III excluded Sudan (except for regional events) and Eritrea, following instructions from GIZ. Phase III has an overall budget of EUR 41 million for all implementing partners.¹¹

Phase III of BMM for IOM commenced on 1 October 2022 and ends on 31 March 2025 (with the majority of activities ended in December 2024). IOM’s work covered Djibouti, Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan, and Uganda, including joint efforts through regional activities that foster cross-country collaboration and coherence across the participating countries.

As per the previous phases, the contract with the donor was signed by GIZ as the lead implementing partner of the BMM. This contract comprises the overall results framework for the whole BMM Phase III. Subsequently, GIZ and IOM agreed on the results framework and indicators applicable to the IOM component of the BMM.¹² The budget for IOM’s work under Phase III was set for 9,855,083.66 EUR. IOM submits its annual reports to GIZ, who subsequently reports to the donor.¹³

The original budget was 9,555,083.56 EUR. IOM received a top up signed in early 2024 providing an additional 300,000 EUR for IOM’s response to Gender-Based Violence and the Sudan crisis. The additional funds went to the Regional Office for: training on Gender-Based Violence (GBV), development of GBV materials, development of the National Referral Mechanism (NRM) (for Djibouti); and to South Sudan to conduct a GBV mapping at the Sudan/South Sudan border and training for frontline protection actors
